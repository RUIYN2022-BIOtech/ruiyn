# Surgical Dressing X pro

**Product Description:**

The world's first oil-hydrogel pad with active suture protection technology.
Surgical Pad Dressing X pro, utilizing advanced biopolymers, is the new generation of specialized
operating room dressings. Its unique oil-hydrogel structure creates a protective barrier against
contaminants and prevents adhesion to sutures and granulation tissue through the gradual release of
moisturizing compounds.

**Features:**

- Oil-Hydrogel Structure: Creates a non-adherent protective layer over sutures + prevents skin
dryness during healing.

- Long-Lasting Moisturization: Releases high-concentration hydrating compounds.

- Anti-Adhesive to Delicate Tissues: Painless separation from granulation and absorbable sutures.

- Controlled Breathability: Optimal oxygen exchange.

- Protective Shield Against Contamination: 99% reduction in bacterial penetration through a dense
biopolymeric matrix.

- Instant Soothing: Reduces post-operative pain with an immediate cooling effect.

- Dynamic Conformity to Skin Movement: 360° flexibility without stretching the wound.

- Diagnostic Transparency: Allows wound monitoring without removing the dressing.

**Indications:**

- **Class I Surgeries:**

Orthopedic surgical incisions (joint replacement, tendon repair)

Cosmetic incisions

Skin sutures after general surgeries

- **Class II High-Risk Surgeries:**

Skin graft donor sites

Skin-sparing breast cancer surgeries

Laparoscopic port sites

- **Specific Cases:**

Split-thickness skin grafts

Suture lines in patients with EDS (Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome)

Surgeries on dry, fragile skin

**Professional Usage Instructions:**

1. After suturing, clean the wound with sterile normal saline.

2. Select a pad 3-4 cm larger than the wound size.

3. Remove the protective liner and place the pad on the wound without stretching.

4. Fix the edges with gentle pressure.

5. Changing Schedule:

Class I Surgery: Post-op days 3, 7, and 14.

Class II Surgery: Post-op days 2, 5, and 10.

6. For moderately exuding surgeries, a secondary dressing is not required.

**Contraindications:**

Active infected wounds

3rd-degree burns

Known hypersensitivity to saturated hydrocarbons

Use on non-debrided necrotic wounds

**Warnings:**

For use by medical personnel only.

Avoid contact with eyes and mucous membranes.

Sterilizable with Ethylene Oxide (Do not autoclave).

Keep away from ignition sources.

Do not refrigerate.

**Storage Conditions:**

Store at room temperature, away from direct sunlight and moisture.

Keep in the original packaging.
