# Wound Gel Neoklean

**Product Description:**

Wound Gel Neoklean is an advanced solution for managing severe infected wounds. Utilizing high-concentration silver nanoparticles, advanced biopolymers, camel collagen, betaine, and glycerin, it creates an aggressive environment against infections. By rapidly inhibiting polymicrobial infections and accelerating the tissue regeneration process, it enables effective healing of complex wounds. Targeted release of silver nanoparticles maintains optimal moisture, prevents biofilm formation, and facilitates autolytic debridement by softening necrotic tissue.

**Features:**

- Powerful Advanced Antimicrobial: Eliminates a broad spectrum of bacteria, fungi, and viruses.
- Camel Collagen: 95% faster absorption than bovine collagen + stimulates human collagen synthesis.
- Smart High-Concentration Nano Release: Controlled release of high-concentration silver nanoparticles (targeted and potent antibacterial activity).
- Wound Matrix Regeneration: Forms a nanostructured scaffold for fibroblast migration and angiogenesis.
- Accelerates Epithelialization: Increases wound closure speed by 60%.
- Dynamic Moisture Manager: Intelligent absorption/transfer of exudate with biopolymers and glycerin.
- Non-Adherent to Wound Bed: Pain-free removal without damaging healing tissues.
- Prevents Biofilm & Infection: Resists microbial colonization within the dressing.
- Active Microbial Barrier: Reduces penetration of microorganisms through the dressing.
- Prevents Wound Dryness: Maintains moisture and forms a protective hydrating layer.
- Necrotic Tissue Softener: Facilitates autolytic debridement.
- Painless Necrosis Removal: Degrades fibrinous matrix.
- Biocompatible: Latex/paraben-free, non-toxic, non-sensitizing.
- Instant Pain Relief: Immediate cooling + reduces inflammation.
- Long-Lasting: Does not require daily changing.
- Secondary Dressing Compatible: Can be used with all absorbent dressings.

**Indications:**

This dressing is designed for managing a wide range of infected or infection-prone acute and chronic wounds.

❖ **Critical Infected Wounds:**

Infected diabetic wounds

Contaminated pressure ulcers (bedsores)

Colonized venous ulcers

❖ **Acute Infection-Prone Wounds:**

Severe and infected burns

Open surgical wounds

Infected wounds

❖ **Treatment-Resistant Wounds:**

Deep fungal wounds

Radiation-induced wounds

Weeping eczema/dermatitis

**General Instructions for Use:**

1. Clean the wound thoroughly.

2. Apply a thin layer of gel to the wound.

3. Cover the wound with a bandage or secondary dressing.

4. Change the dressing and apply fresh gel as directed by a doctor.

**Contraindications:**

Do not use if there is a known hypersensitivity to any component of Wound Gel X pro NANO. Not for use
on wounds with very low exudate (dry wounds). Mixing this gel with other soaps, lotions, ointments,
oils, or enzymes may reduce efficacy. Do not use in combination with anionic surfactants.

**Warnings:**

Keep out of reach of children. For external use on wounds only. Do not use for injection. Do not use in
the middle or inner ear. Do not use in the eyes.

**Storage Conditions:**

Store at room temperature away from direct sunlight.

Protect from freezing.
