# Wound Solution Argentum

**Product Description:**

Wound Solution Argentum is an advanced combination for deep cleaning and disinfecting acute and chronic wounds. Utilizing silver nanoparticles and betaine, it mechanically cleanses the wound environment of pathogens while providing gentle irrigation. Targeted release of silver nanoparticles prevents biofilm formation and accelerates healing by preserving the integrity of healthy tissues. The smart betaine-based formulation prevents chemical irritation and prepares the wound for subsequent dressing stages.

**Features:**

- Potent Targeted Disinfectant: Controlled release of silver nanoparticles (eliminates bacteria, fungi, and viruses without harming fibroblasts).
- Gentle Compatible Cleanser: Mechanical irrigation without abrasion of sensitive tissues (aided by betaine's surface properties).
- Prevents Biofilm Formation: Deep penetration into wound layers inhibiting microbial colonization.
- Preserves Wound Ecosystem: Does not interfere with natural wound pH and maintains required cellular moisture.
- Anti-Inflammatory & Soothing: Immediate reduction of swelling and pain caused by contamination.
- High Biocompatibility: Alcohol/iodine/paraben-free, non-toxic, non-sensitizing.
- Improves Dressing Efficacy: Prepares the wound for optimal absorption of gels or secondary dressings.
- No Rinse Required: Leaves no particles or residue on the wound after use.

**Indications:**

This solution is designed for irrigating a wide range of wounds:

✦ **Contaminated/Infected Wounds:**

Colonized diabetic or venous ulcers

Pressure ulcers with purulent exudate

Infected burns

✦ **Acute Infection-Prone Wounds:**

Open surgical wounds

Deep traumatic lacerations

Extensive abrasions

✦ **Complex Wounds:**

Animal bite wounds

Infected penetrating wounds

Infected weeping dermatitis

**General Instructions for Use:**

1. Irrigate the wound with a gentle stream of the solution.

2. Repeat 2-3 times for deep cleansing.

3. After irrigation, dry the wound with sterile gauze and apply the primary dressing.

4. Repeat irrigation based on wound type and physician instructions.

**Contraindications:**

Do not use if there is a known hypersensitivity to any component. Mixing this solution with other agents
may reduce efficacy. Do not use with anionic surfactants.

**Warnings:**

Keep out of reach of children. For external use on wounds only. Do not use for injection. Do not use in
the middle or inner ear. Do not use in the eyes.

**Storage Conditions:**

Store at room temperature away from direct sunlight.

Protect from freezing.
