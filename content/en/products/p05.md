# High-Tech Dressing Spray

**Product Description:**

A world's first innovation. After applying the accompanying gel to the wound, this spray is applied, triggering a polymerization process. This creates a polymerized structure that mimics the properties of a cellular scaffold, leading to significantly faster wound healing and protecting the wound from microorganisms and contaminants. This layer has self-regulating moisture properties, controlling wound humidity based on exudate levels. The formed layer is transparent, allowing wound visualization. This dressing reduces local inflammation, absorbs exudate, provides complete wound coverage, maintains optimal moisture, and treats infection.  
High-Tech Dressing Spray has strong antimicrobial effects and a long-lasting antimicrobial formulation. It simulates the properties of a cellular scaffold.

**Features:**

- No Secondary Dressing Required
- Smart Nano Release: Controlled release of silver nanoparticles (targeted antibacterial activity).
- Dynamic Moisture Manager: Intelligent absorption/transfer of exudate with an advanced hydrogel structure.
- Non-Adherent to Wound Bed: Pain-free removal without damaging healing tissues.
- Prevents Biofilm & Infection: Resists microbial colonization within the dressing.
- Active Microbial Barrier: Reduces penetration of microorganisms through the dressing.
- Necrotic Tissue Softener: Facilitates autolytic debridement.
- Prevents Wound Dryness: Maintains moisture and forms a protective hydrating layer.
- Biocompatible: Latex/paraben-free, non-toxic, non-sensitizing.
- Instant Pain Relief: Immediate cooling + reduces inflammation.
- Long-Lasting: Does not require daily changing.
- Simulates Cellular Scaffold Properties.

**Indications:**

This dressing is designed for managing a wide range of acute and chronic wounds.

✦ **Chronic Wounds:**

Diabetic ulcers  
Pressure ulcers (bedsores)  
Venous leg ulcers  

✦ **Acute Wounds:**

Burns  
Surgical wounds  
Lacerations, cuts, and abrasions from trauma

❖ **Complex Wounds:**

Infected wounds

Radiation-induced wounds

Weeping eczema/dermatitis

**General Instructions for Use:**

1. Clean the wound thoroughly.

2. Apply a thin layer of the accompanying gel to the wound bed.

3. Spray the High-Tech Spray onto the gel.

4. Allow it to dry gently after 5 seconds.

5. Reapply based on exudate levels and physician instructions.

**Contraindications:**

Do not use if there is a known hypersensitivity to any component of Wound Gel X pro NANO. Not for use
on wounds with very low exudate (dry wounds). Mixing this gel with other soaps, lotions, ointments,
oils, or enzymes may reduce efficacy. Do not use in combination with anionic surfactants.

**Warnings:**

Keep out of reach of children. For external use on wounds only. Do not use for injection. Do not use in
the middle or inner ear. Do not use in the eyes.

**Storage Conditions:**

Store at room temperature away from direct sunlight.

Protect from freezing.
