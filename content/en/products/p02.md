# Pad Dressing X pro NANO Silver

**Product Description:**

Hydrogel Dressing X pro NANO utilizes advanced biopolymers and silver nanoparticles in flexible pads to create an optimal environment for cell regeneration. It maintains wound moisture, softens necrotic tissue, facilitates autolytic debridement, prevents dryness and secondary damage, and enables faster, scar-free healing. Controlled release of silver nanoparticles prevents biofilm formation and infection.

**Features:**

- Smart Nano Release: Controlled release of silver nanoparticles (targeted antibacterial activity).
- Dynamic Moisture Manager: Intelligent absorption/transfer of exudate with an advanced hydrogel structure.
- Non-Adherent to Wound Bed: Pain-free removal without damaging healing tissues.
- Prevents Biofilm & Infection: Resists microbial colonization within the dressing.
- Active Microbial Barrier: Reduces penetration of microorganisms through the dressing.
- Necrotic Tissue Softener: Facilitates autolytic debridement.
- Prevents Wound Dryness: Maintains constant moisture with a protective layer formation.
- Biocompatible: Latex/paraben-free, non-toxic, non-sensitizing.
- Instant Pain & Inflammation Relief: Immediate cooling + reduces inflammation.
- Long-Lasting: Does not require daily changing.
- Secondary Dressing Compatible: Can be used with all absorbent dressings.
- Conforms to Irregular Wounds: Can be cut to shape without structural loss.

**Indications:**

**This dressing is designed for managing a wide range of acute and chronic wounds.**

❖ **Chronic Wounds:**

Diabetic ulcers

Pressure ulcers (bedsores)

Venous leg ulcers

❖ **Acute Wounds:**

Burns

Surgical wounds

Lacerations, cuts, and abrasions from trauma

❖ **Complex Wounds:**

Infected wounds

Radiation-induced wounds

Weeping eczema/dermatitis

**General Instructions for Use:**

1. Clean the wound thoroughly.

2. Select or cut the pad 2-3 cm larger than the wound.

3. Remove the protective liner and place the pad directly onto the wound.

4. Cover the wound with a bandage or secondary dressing.

5. Change the dressing based on exudate levels and physician instructions.

**Contraindications:**

Do not use if there is a known hypersensitivity to any component of Wound Gel X pro NANO. Not for use
on wounds with very low exudate (dry wounds). Mixing this gel with other soaps, lotions, ointments,
oils, or enzymes may reduce efficacy. Do not use in combination with anionic surfactants.

**Warnings:**

Keep out of reach of children. For external use on wounds only.

**Storage Conditions:**

Store at room temperature away from direct sunlight.

Protect from freezing.
